A Legacy of Craftsmanship & Trust

Established in 2010 under the expert leadership of Ahmed Raza Siddiqui, Royal For Ceiling (Ahmed Royal Ceiling) has been at the forefront of bespoke architectural ceiling solutions across Telangana for over 16 years.

We specialize in Gypsum False Ceilings, POP Design, Wooden Baffles, Armstrong Grid Ceilings, and Profile Lighting. All our projects feature termite-proof materials, master craftsmanship, and a 2-Year After-Service Guarantee for your complete peace of mind.
